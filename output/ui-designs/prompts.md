Home headline "Your next favourite bite." with "favourite" orange; a wide immersive hero photo of sizzling grilled chicken with dark left gradient and "Fresh off the grill." overlay. Food photos dominate the menu cards. Detail screen is rich and cinematic but has high-contrast readable content. A tiny orange line above screen labels. Restrained premium typography, clean sans serif.
