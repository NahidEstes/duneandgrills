# Stock alert concepts

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. These are explanatory mockups with sample data, not implemented screens.

## Shared visual direction

High-resolution landscape UI concepts for Dune & Grills Inventory. Near-black and charcoal surfaces, amber primary controls, sand accents, readable English UI and Bengali explanatory headings. Flat desktop framing with no perspective. Explicitly label every design as a concept with sample data.

## 01 - Alert setup

Show item-specific toggles, a selected Burger Sauce rule, a 1.0 kg usable-stock threshold, stock-out notification, a reminder every 30 minutes while unresolved, selected recipients, and a Save rule button. Show another healthy item and one item with alerts disabled. Explain choosing the item, threshold and interval.

## 02 - Notifications

Show Burger Sauce at 0.8 kg against a 1.0 kg threshold, a first alert at 10:00 AM, and a reminder at 10:30 AM if still low. Include View stock and Snooze controls. Show healthy stock without alerts and an explicitly separate alternative scenario for zero usable stock. Disabled items do not receive notifications from these rules.

## 03 - Recovery

Four-step timeline: 10:00 AM stock is 0.8 kg and first alert is sent; 10:30 AM stock is still low and one reminder is sent; 10:45 AM actual production is recorded and stock reaches 2.5 kg; 11:00 AM there is no reminder because stock is above 1.0 kg. A future drop begins a new incident. Alerts themselves do not change inventory.
