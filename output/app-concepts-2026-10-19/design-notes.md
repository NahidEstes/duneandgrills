# Restaurant notebook UI concepts
Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. These are visual concepts.

Prompt set:
- Shared: High-fidelity portrait smartphone UI for a Bengali restaurant quick bookkeeping notebook. Dune & Grills wordmark, warm ivory surfaces, forest-green primary controls, amber pending status, Bengali text, SAR amounts. Notes do not automatically change inventory.
- 01-home.png: Home screen with pending count, today's expenses, prominent new-note button, recent purchase and expense cards, and bottom navigation.
- 02-add-note.png: Quick free-text note form with optional category, receipt photo, amount, payment method, and save button. Example: chicken 10 kg, 230 SAR, paid cash.
- 03-review-note.png: Note details with quantity, amount, payment, attached receipt, pending status, edit action, and a button marking that manual entry into the main accounts is complete.
